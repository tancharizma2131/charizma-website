About Charizma

Charizma Management is a boutique model agency dedicated to discovering distinctive talent and building careers through personal guidance, thoughtful development, and international placements.

Founded in 2024 by models Tan Patipan and Vasilina Panina, Charizma draws on more than a decade of combined experience in the modelling industry across Asia, Europe, and America. Having navigated the industry ourselves, we understand the importance of finding the right opportunities, adapting to different markets, and having someone in your corner.

We work closely with each model to develop their portfolio, prepare for placements, and connect with agencies that suit their strengths. Honest conversations and a clear sense of direction guide our approach, with each person’s individuality and ambitions at its heart.

Our aim is to build lasting relationships—with our models and our agency partners—and give every career we help shape room to grow.
