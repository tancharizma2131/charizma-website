About Charizma

Founded in 2024 by professional models Tan Patipan and Vasilina Panina — Charizma Management was born from a shared passion for individuality and a belief in building honest, strategic careers in fashion.

With years of experience across Asia, Europe, and America — including campaigns for Louis Vuitton, Vogue, and castings for major Fashion Weeks in New York, London, Milan, and Paris — Tan and Vasilina bring real-world insight into the challenges and opportunities models face. They've lived the journey from the inside, and now use that experience to mentor and guide new talent.

Charizma is a boutique agency committed to discovering and nurturing distinctive models from around the globe, connecting them with forward-thinking agencies worldwide. Through a transparent and collaborative approach, we build tailored development plans that celebrate individuality and elevate potential.

At Charizma, we believe individuality is power — and that the future of fashion is personal.
