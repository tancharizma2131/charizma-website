About Charizma

Charizma Management is a boutique model agency discovering distinctive talent and developing careers through personal guidance and international placements.

We work closely with each model to build their portfolio, prepare for new markets, and connect with agencies suited to their strengths. Our approach begins with the individual: their character, ambitions, and the opportunities that make sense for their career.

The people behind Charizma

Founded in 2024 by models Tan Patipan and Vasilina Panina, Charizma draws on more than 10 years of combined modelling experience across Asia, Europe, and America. That firsthand experience shapes the guidance we offer, from preparing for a first placement to navigating a new market.
